# Harper Lane Films — Episode 001: Character Bible

Canonical character sheets for "Before I Let You Go" — all 6 named characters. Transcribed from
the finished character sheet designs (Josephine's-branded, "GOOD FOOD. STRONGER PEOPLE." tagline
established as the restaurant's own slogan — worth reusing on thumbnail/description copy).

**One correction from the source sheets**: Dani's sheet described Josephine's as "her late
mother's restaurant." Per the locked story, that's wrong — Patricia (the mother) is alive and
present throughout, and Josephine is the deceased *grandmother* the restaurant is named after,
who Patricia originally took it over from. Corrected below; the sheets' own Patricia page (alive,
present in Scene 12 comforting Dani) confirms this was the tool's phrasing slip, not a real
premise change.

Each sheet's photo set already functions as the turnaround/consistency reference for that
character — multiple angles, expressions, and outfit variants generated together. Use these as
the image-to-image source for any new shot of that character instead of re-prompting from text.

## Danielle "Dani" Carter — Lead

**Age:** Late 30s
**Occupation:** Chef / Restaurant Owner (Josephine's)
**Family:** Daughter of Patricia, sister of Isaiah, wife of Marcus (at the beginning)
**Personality:** Practical, resilient, observant, warm, hardworking, emotionally honest. Not
easily shaken, but deeply feels the weight of betrayal.
**Core arc:** Rebuilding → Discovery → Betrayal → Confrontation → Independence
**Tagline:** "Sometimes the only way to keep the thing that matters is deciding what you're
willing to let go of first."
**Key traits:** Resilient and hardworking. Deeply values family and legacy. Practical and
solution-oriented. Slow to anger but direct when pushed. Emotionally intelligent. Loyal, but not
blindly. Finds strength in action, not words. Learns to choose herself.
**Story overview:** After two difficult years, Dani reopens her grandmother's restaurant,
Josephine's, hoping to rebuild her life. What should be a fresh start turns into a deeper fight
when she discovers her husband Marcus has secretly taken loans against Josephine's to fund his
gambling. As the truth unravels, Dani must decide what to save, what to let go, and who she can
truly rely on. By the end, she reclaims her restaurant, her name on the paperwork, and her sense
of self.
**Wardrobe (5 looks):** Working at Josephine's (primary look — dark apron over a cream henley);
casual/early scenes; home look; confrontation scenes; later/moving forward.
**Key relationships:** Marcus (husband, at the beginning — love, betrayal, difficult truth);
Renee (best friend — honest, supportive, keeps Dani grounded); Patricia (mother — emotional
anchor, strength and wisdom); Isaiah (brother — blunt but caring, wants what's best for her).
**Key objects:** Josephine's apron (grandmother's, mother's, now hers); restaurant keys (a new
beginning); the default notice (the truth on paper); the framed notice (a reminder of what she no
longer tolerates).

## Marcus Carter — Husband

**Age:** Late 30s – early 40s
**Occupation:** Corporate professional (finance/business)
**Family:** Husband of Dani, brother-in-law to Isaiah, son-in-law to Patricia (by marriage)
**Personality:** Charming, intelligent, ambitious, conflict-avoidant, good at appearing in
control. Struggles with shame and addiction.
**Core conflict:** Gambling addiction, mounting debt, and a growing web of lies he's afraid to
face.
**Arc:** Love → Secrecy → Deeper Lies → Consequences → Loss → Reckoning (future uncertain)
**Tagline:** "He loved her. But he loved the next win more."
**Key traits:** Charismatic and likeable. Ambitious and driven. Skilled at deflection. Deeply
ashamed of his addiction. Loves Dani, but makes selfish choices. Good at appearing composed.
Avoids difficult conversations. Acts out of fear and pride. Not inherently a bad man, but causes
real harm. Faces real consequences.
**Story overview:** Marcus is a successful, well-spoken professional married to Dani. On the
surface, he's supportive, loving, and proud of her return to Josephine's. Behind closed doors,
he's been gambling for three years, taking out loans — including one against Josephine's —
without telling Dani. He convinces himself he'll win it back before she ever finds out, but his
choices only make things worse, ultimately costing him her trust, their marriage, and his place
in her life.
**Wardrobe (5 looks):** Work attire (office/professional); casual (early scenes); at Josephine's
(supportive visit); home (argument scenes); leaving (end of marriage).
**Key relationships:** Dani (wife — love of his life, hurt by his lies, eventually walks away);
Patricia (mother-in-law — respects her, has let her down); Isaiah (brother-in-law — tense but
honest relationship); Renee (sees through him, not trusting); Devon (gambling contact — a
dangerous connection from his past).
**Key objects:** Phone (constant calls, ignored messages, secrets); gambling chips/cards (his
addiction and downfall); loan documents (took loans without Dani's knowledge); car keys (leaving
home, a turning point); small bag (not much in it — the end of a chapter).

## Renee Bennett — Best Friend / Confidante

**Age:** Mid 30s
**Occupation:** Marketing & Events Coordinator (freelance)
**Family:** Chosen family — considers Dani a sister.
**Personality:** Honest, supportive, witty, loyal, perceptive, protective, practical. Tells the
truth even when it's uncomfortable.
**Core role:** Dani's confidante and reality check. Notices what Dani misses, helps uncover the
truth, and reminds Dani of her strength.
**Tagline:** "Sometimes the most loving thing you can do is tell the truth."
**Key traits:** Loyal and dependable. Speaks her mind. Emotionally intelligent. Supportive without
enabling. Observant — notices the small things. Witty and brings levity. Protective of Dani. Good
at practical problem-solving. Values honesty over comfort. Believes in Dani's independence.
**Story overview:** Renee is Dani's best friend and unwavering support system. She's the one who
shows up with coffee, asks the hard questions, and sees the red flags long before Dani is ready.
Renee balances warmth with tough love — she celebrates Dani's wins, helps her investigate Marcus's
financial secrets, and refuses to let her stay in denial. Through every stage, Renee is a constant
reminder that Dani is not alone, and that she deserves honesty, peace, and more.
**Wardrobe (5 looks):** Casual (everyday look); at Josephine's (working with Dani); investigating
(serious moments); professional (work); casual home (with friends). Signature: bold colorful
print top, gold hoop earrings.
**Key relationships:** Dani (best friend — like a sister, fiercely supportive, always honest with
her); Marcus (Dani's husband — doesn't trust him, sees through his excuses); Patricia (respects
her, supports Dani's bond with her mother); Isaiah (keeps it real — sometimes allies, sometimes
challenges); Devon (wary of him, knows he's part of Marcus's past).
**Key objects:** Coffee (shows up with "reinforcements"); laptop (research, bank statements,
evidence); notebook (always prepared, keeps track); phone (calls, check-ins, finding the truth);
tote bag (work, life, everything in one).

## Isaiah Carter — Younger Brother

**Age:** Mid to late 20s
**Occupation:** Between jobs / exploring next steps (formerly various roles)
**Family:** Son of Patricia, brother to Dani (biological), brother-in-law to Marcus (by marriage)
**Personality:** Honest, protective, loyal, frustrated, empathetic, blunt but caring, still
figuring out his own path.
**Core role:** Challenges Dani, tells hard truths, pushes her to consider letting go, ultimately
supports her and steps up when it matters.
**Arc:** Critic → Conflict → Reflection → Support → Growth
**Tagline:** "Sometimes love sounds like what you don't want to hear."
**Key traits:** Loyal and protective. Blunt, sometimes too blunt. Deeply cares about Dani. Sees
the bigger picture. Struggles with his own direction. Honest and self-aware (eventually). Willing
to admit when he's wrong. Steps up when it matters. Values family over ego. Grows through the
experience.
**Story overview:** Isaiah is Dani's younger brother and one of the few people who speaks to her
with unfiltered honesty. He sees what Marcus is doing before Dani is ready to admit it, and he
pushes her to consider selling Josephine's and walking away — not because he doesn't value the
restaurant, but because he doesn't want to see her lose everything. Their relationship is tested
when Dani calls him out on his own lack of direction, which leads to a deeper, more honest
understanding between them. By the end, Isaiah shows up — not just with opinions, but with real
support, helping at the restaurant and proving he's learned how to be there in more than just
words.
**Wardrobe (5 looks):** Casual (everyday); casual out (with friends); at Josephine's (helping
out); family meeting (serious conversations); restaurant work (supporting Dani, apron).
**Key relationships:** Dani (sister — loves and protects her, speaks hard truths, wants what's
best for her); Patricia (mother — respects her, sometimes butts heads, but they're close); Marcus
(brother-in-law — never fully trusted him, calls out the red flags); Renee (respects her, often
aligned in wanting the truth).
**Key objects:** Headphones (for space, music, and thinking); notebook (ideas, goals, a better
future); keys (helps at Josephine's, being part of the solution); coffee mug (long conversations,
family, life, what's next); family photo (a reminder of what — and who — matters).

## Patricia Carter — Mother

**Age:** Early 60s
**Occupation:** Retired Restaurateur / Family Matriarch
**Family:** Mother of Dani, mother-in-law to Marcus, mother to Isaiah (by marriage)
**Personality:** Wise, practical, compassionate, strong-willed, observant, calm under pressure,
protective.
**Core role:** Emotional anchor. Keeper of family legacy. Provides truth, encouragement, and
perspective. Reminds Dani of her strength and the history behind Josephine's.
**Tagline:** "Roots run deep. So do we."
**Key traits:** Strong, resilient, and grounded. Wise and experienced. Protective of Dani. Speaks
with honesty and compassion. Calm in crisis. Believes in accountability. Values family, legacy,
and hard work. Sees the bigger picture. Offers tough love when needed. Faithful, hopeful, and
steady.
**Story overview:** Patricia Carter is Dani's mother and the heart of the Carter family. She took
over Josephine's from Dani's grandmother and kept it alive through tough times — a divorce,
recessions, and even a kitchen fire. She represents resilience, legacy, and unconditional love.
Patricia supports Dani's return to the restaurant, offers honest advice when Marcus's betrayal
comes to light, and helps Dani find the strength to rebuild. She doesn't sugarcoat the truth, but
she always leads with love.
**Wardrobe (5 looks):** Josephine's (work apron); casual home (everyday comfort); family
gathering (church/formal); casual day (running errands); at home (comfort and care).
**Key relationships:** Dani (daughter — protects and guides her, believes in her strength);
Marcus (son-in-law — sees his potential but not afraid to hold him accountable); Isaiah (son, by
marriage — loves him like her own, keeps him grounded); Renee (Dani's friend — welcomes her and
trusts her judgment); Josephine (legacy, deceased — her example lives on through the restaurant).
**Key objects:** Josephine's apron (a legacy of strength and survival); family photo (three
generations, same strength); recipe book (history, love, and tradition); coffee mug (for tough
conversations); fresh flowers (a reminder that there's always another season).

## Devon — Single-Scene (Scene 10 only)

**Age:** Early to mid 40s
**Occupation:** High-stakes gambler (fixer/underground network)
**Family:** Unknown (keeps his personal life private)
**Personality:** Street-smart, guarded, observant, pragmatic, low tolerance for foolishness, more
principled than he admits.
**Core role:** Marcus's connection to the gambling world. Provides the loan, represents the
consequences of Marcus's addiction.
**Arc:** Enabler → Warning → Reluctant Assistance → Draws the Line
**Tagline:** "I've seen this story before."
**Key traits:** World-weary and perceptive. Skeptical of Marcus's excuses. Direct and no-nonsense.
Not easily manipulated. Has a code, even in a gray world. Knows the cost of gambling firsthand.
Blunt, but not cruel. Gives tough love. Protective in his own way. Sets clear boundaries.
**Story overview:** Devon is Marcus's old friend and a key figure from his gambling past. He has
likely bailed Marcus out before and knows exactly how the cycle works. When Marcus shows up again,
claiming he's done and just needs a loan to cover a mortgage payment, Devon is skeptical but
ultimately gives him the money — with harsh terms. He warns Marcus that this is the last time.
Devon is not a villain — he represents the world Marcus keeps running back to, and the very real
consequences of addiction, bad decisions, and borrowed time.
**Wardrobe:** Casual (everyday); at the bar (primary look); street/on the go (layers); serious
conversation (with Marcus); low profile (baseball cap, discreet).
**Key relationships:** Marcus (old friend — tries to help, but no longer trusts him, sees the
pattern); Dani (doesn't interact directly, but understands the impact of his involvement); Renee
(likely unaware of him, represents the kind of honesty Marcus avoids); Patricia (doesn't
interact — represents what Marcus risks losing); Isaiah (doesn't interact — would likely distrust
him if they met).
**Key objects:** Cash (a temporary solution, a dangerous one); loan agreement (terms with a
cost); poker chips (the beginning of the problem); whiskey (a familiar setting, familiar
choices); phone (the call that brings Marcus back); the bar (where the past always seems to find
him).
