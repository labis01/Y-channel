# Harper Lane Films — Episode 001: "Before I Let You Go"

## Status
Concept locked. Title locked. Story treatment below. Not yet scripted scene-by-scene.

## Why this title
Modeled directly on Grace Studios' outlier — "BEFORE I LET YOU GO | THE COMPLETE MOVIE"
(1.92M views, published Jul 26 2026), the single video responsible for most of their 40.9K subs
off only 6 uploads. Reusing the exact title deliberately: it's a common phrase (also a Frankie
Beverly & Maze / Cynthia Erivo song title, not a distinctive trademark), so there's no de-risking
conflict, and it's a proven high-performing hook in this exact content category. The story under
it is original — same emotional formula (betrayal → devastating loss → choice to rebuild) as
Imani's arc, different plot, different characters, same house genre (Black/African-American
relationship & family drama, dialogue-driven, multiple voices).

## Logline
After burying her mother, a woman discovers the family restaurant she just inherited has been
secretly remortgaged by her husband to cover a gambling debt he's hidden for years — and has one
week before the bank forecloses to decide what she's actually fighting to save: the marriage, or
herself.

## Why this plot (not a retelling of Imani's)
Grace Studios' version centers infidelity as the betrayal. This version centers financial
secrecy/gambling — avoids the most well-worn version of the trope, still delivers the same
betrayal → loss → rebuild shape, and ties the stakes to something concrete and visual (a physical
place, a legacy, a foreclosure deadline) rather than an abstract relationship state — gives the
40-minute runtime a ticking-clock structure to hang scenes on.

## Cast & voice needs (ElevenLabs — multiple voices required, distinct from Haven Sands)
- **Danielle "Dani" Carter** — lead. Inherits her late mother's soul-food restaurant. Warm but
  resolute; carries most of the runtime. Needs a voice with range for quiet
  grief and hardened resolve, not just one note.
- **Marcus Carter** — husband. Hid a years-long gambling debt by secretly remortgaging the
  restaurant. Remorseful-but-evasive tone — not a cartoon villain, a man who kept digging the
  hole deeper because he couldn't admit the first lie.
- **Renee** — Dani's best friend/confidante. Grounded, direct — the audience's reality-check
  voice. Distinct timbre from Dani so scenes between them read clearly without visual cues.
- **Isaiah** — Dani's younger brother. Wants to sell the restaurant and cash out now that their
  mother's gone. Source of family friction, not a villain — a legitimate, different read on grief.
- **Patricia (deceased)** — Dani's mother. Voice-only, in a flashback or a saved voicemail beat.
  Older, warmer register.

## Structure (40 min target)
1. **0:00–10:00 — Cold open / setup.** Funeral. The restaurant sits closed, dusty, in disarray.
   On the drive home, a foreclosure notice arrives in the mail — first crack in a marriage that
   otherwise looks stable on the surface.
2. **10:00–25:00 — The betrayal unravels.** Dani confronts Marcus; learns the debt goes back
   years, hidden behind small lies that compounded. Isaiah pushes to just sell and split what's
   left. Renee starts digging into exactly what Marcus signed away and when.
3. **25:00–35:00 — Rock bottom.** Marcus tries to fix it himself — one more bet, one more
   scheme — and makes it worse. The foreclosure becomes real and immediate. Dani is forced to
   actually choose, not just be angry.
4. **35:00–40:00 — The "before I let you go" beat.** Dani lets go — of the marriage, or of the
   version of it she was still holding onto — to save the restaurant on her own terms. Bittersweet
   resolution, not a rescue: family and community rally around her, but nothing is fully "fixed."
