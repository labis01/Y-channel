# Harper Lane Films — Episode 001: "Before I Let You Go"

## Status
Concept locked. Title locked. Story treatment below. Not yet scripted scene-by-scene.

## Why this title
Modeled directly on Grace Studios' outlier — "BEFORE I LET YOU GO | THE COMPLETE MOVIE"
(1.92M views, published Jul 26 2026), the single video responsible for most of their 40.9K subs
off only 6 uploads. Reusing the exact title deliberately: it's a common phrase (also a Frankie
Beverly & Maze / Cynthia Erivo song title, not a distinctive trademark), so there's no de-risking
conflict, and it's a proven high-performing hook in this exact content category. The story under
it is original — same emotional formula (betrayal → devastating loss → choice to rebuild) as
Imani's arc, different plot, different characters, same house genre (Black/African-American
relationship & family drama, dialogue-driven, multiple voices).

## Logline
Reopening the restaurant her mother handed her as a fresh start after a miscarriage, a woman
discovers her husband secretly remortgaged it to cover a gambling debt he's hidden for years —
and has one week before the bank forecloses to decide what she's actually fighting to save: the
marriage, or herself.

**No death, no funeral.** The "devastating loss" is a miscarriage Dani suffered months earlier,
which she's still quietly grieving when the betrayal surfaces — nobody in the story dies. Patricia
(Dani's mother) is alive and present throughout, not a deceased/flashback character.

**Visual constraint — locked, applies to script and every image/video prompt:** no hospital
scenes, no blood, no depiction of loss of life. The miscarriage is handled entirely through
dialogue and performance (a line, a silence, a look) — never shown, never flashed back to as an
event, no medical setting or imagery anywhere in the story. Grief plays out in ordinary spaces
(the restaurant, the kitchen, the car) through what characters say and don't say, not through
depicting the loss itself.

## Why this plot (not a retelling of Imani's)
Grace Studios' version centers infidelity as the betrayal, and a death as the loss. This version
centers financial secrecy/gambling as the betrayal and a miscarriage as the loss — avoids the two
most well-worn versions of the trope, still delivers the same betrayal → loss → rebuild shape, and
ties the stakes to something concrete and visual (a physical place, a fresh-start gift from her
mother, a foreclosure deadline) rather than an abstract relationship state. Grieving privately
while the financial betrayal unravels raises the emotional stakes of the confrontation scenes
without needing a death in the story at all.

## Cast & voice needs (ElevenLabs — multiple voices required, distinct from Haven Sands)
- **Danielle "Dani" Carter** — lead. Reopening the restaurant her mother gave her as a fresh
  start after her miscarriage. Warm but resolute; carries most of the runtime. Needs a voice with
  range for quiet grief and hardened resolve, not just one note.
- **Marcus Carter** — husband. Hid a years-long gambling debt by secretly remortgaging the
  restaurant. Remorseful-but-evasive tone — not a cartoon villain, a man who kept digging the
  hole deeper because he couldn't admit the first lie.
- **Renee** — Dani's best friend/confidante. Grounded, direct — the audience's reality-check
  voice. Distinct timbre from Dani so scenes between them read clearly without visual cues.
- **Isaiah** — Dani's younger brother. Skeptical of Marcus, pushes Dani to just walk away and
  sell rather than fight for either the marriage or the restaurant. Source of family friction,
  not a villain.
- **Patricia** — Dani's mother, alive and present. Gave Dani the restaurant to run as a reason
  to get out of bed after the miscarriage. Worried, protective, occasionally overbearing —
  a live character in scenes, not a voice-only flashback.

## Structure (40 min target)
1. **0:00–10:00 — Cold open / setup.** Dani reopens the restaurant for its first day back —
   quiet, hopeful, her first real step since the miscarriage. That same day, a foreclosure notice
   arrives. First crack in a marriage that otherwise looks stable on the surface.
2. **10:00–25:00 — The betrayal unravels.** Dani confronts Marcus; learns the debt goes back
   years, hidden behind small lies that compounded — including through the months she was
   grieving, when he chose to protect the secret over telling her. Isaiah pushes her to just
   walk away. Renee starts digging into exactly what Marcus signed away and when.
3. **25:00–35:00 — Rock bottom.** Marcus tries to fix it himself — one more bet, one more
   scheme — and makes it worse. The foreclosure becomes real and immediate. Dani is forced to
   actually choose, not just be angry.
4. **35:00–40:00 — The "before I let you go" beat.** Dani lets go — of the marriage, or of the
   version of it she was still holding onto — to keep running the restaurant on her own terms.
   Bittersweet resolution, not a rescue: Patricia, Renee, and the community rally around her, but
   nothing is fully "fixed."
