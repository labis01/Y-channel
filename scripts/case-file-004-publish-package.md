## Description

Vought International doesn't have a person to catch. It has a payroll.

For three case files now, L has been hunting individuals — Spider-Man, Wolverine, Batman. This
time the target is a corporation, and that changes everything about how the case gets built. No
single fingerprint. No one suspect who can crack under pressure. Just thousands of employees who
each know one small piece of the truth.

So can L actually close a case with no person at the center of it? Turns out the answer isn't as
simple as "no."

This one traces three categories of evidence — physical, digital, and behavioral — through
Vought's pattern of cover-ups, before landing on the one thing an institution can't fake: the
moment somebody inside had to improvise.
