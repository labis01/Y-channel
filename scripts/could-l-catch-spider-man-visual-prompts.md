## HOW TO USE

**Style suffix.** Paste this at the end of EVERY AI prompt so the shots match your cold-open images:
> cinematic film still, photorealistic, 35mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, New York at night, rain, deep navy-blue shadows with warm amber practical lights, wet reflective surfaces, subtle film grain, moody, 16:9

**L's room suffix.** Add this for any shot of L working:
> dark room lit only by a wall of computer monitors, cold blue glow, cluttered desk with sugar cubes and teacups

**Characters.** Copy these descriptions word for word every time, and upload one of your cold-open images as a reference so the faces stay the same.
- **PETER:** young man around 18, messy curly dark-brown hair, tired brown eyes, worn black hoodie under a dark jacket.
- **L:** pale young man, wild messy black hair falling over his eyes, dark circles, loose white long-sleeve shirt, faded jeans, barefoot, crouches on chairs with his knees up, holds things with his fingertips.
- **MJ:** young woman, curly dark hair tied back, barista apron over a dark sweater.
- **HAPPY:** heavyset man in his 50s, dark wool coat.
- **TECH:** lanky 19-year-old with glasses and a repair-shop polo shirt.
- **IMPERSONATOR:** a man in a cheap, baggy spider costume with slightly wrong proportions.
- **HERO:** a masked hero in a red and dark-blue suit with black web lines. Use back views, silhouettes and long shots. If a tool refuses "Spider-Man", use exactly this description.

**Free stock sites:** Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit, Coverr. All are free and need no credit.

**Free CapCut overlays:** rain, film grain, a VHS/CCTV glitch for any "camera footage" shot, and a red REC dot with a timestamp.

**Text in images:** AI tools garble text. Add notes, news headlines and timestamps in CapCut or Canva.

**No movie clips.** Use AI images and stock only. That keeps you clear of reused-content and copyright problems.
