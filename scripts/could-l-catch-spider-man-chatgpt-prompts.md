## How ChatGPT is different from Grok
- **It remembers the chat.** Paste the SETUP message once, then send each shot as a short prompt. Start a new chat about every 20 images (ChatGPT starts drifting after that) and paste SETUP again.
- **It blocks Spider-Man and real actors.** Don't type "Spider-Man", and don't upload your Peter images if they look like a real actor; it'll refuse. The SETUP below describes original characters instead.
- **It's good at text.** Shots marked ✍️ can include the words right in the image (sticky notes, headlines, timestamps).
- **If it makes a square image,** reply: `Make it 16:9 widescreen.`
- **If it refuses a HERO shot,** reply: `Show the HERO as a silhouette from behind.`
