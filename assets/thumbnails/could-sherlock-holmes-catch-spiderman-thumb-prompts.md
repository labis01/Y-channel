# Thumbnail prompt — "Could Sherlock Holmes Catch Spider-Man?"

For use with an external AI image tool (Grok, ChatGPT/DALL-E, Midjourney, etc.)
— this repo doesn't have image-generation access, so this is meant to be
pasted into whichever tool produces the final art.

Matches the reference channel's detailed anime/digital-painting art quality
(the polished shading and linework, not the flatter code-drawn placeholder
in this same folder). Traits are described generically rather than naming
characters directly, since both Grok and ChatGPT rejected prompts that
named the IPs outright as copyright violations. The right-side portrait
is given Holmes-specific props (deerstalker silhouette, pipe, aquiline
nose) as the twist that keeps it from reading as a straight copy of the
reference image.

## Main concept
Text overlay: none

> Highly detailed anime/digital painting style YouTube thumbnail, 1280x720,
> dramatic cel-shading and sharp linework. Symmetrical split-face
> composition divided by a hard vertical seam. Left half: a close-up of a
> solid red full-face mask with large white lens-shaped eyes and fine
> black web-line texture, dramatic rim lighting, set against a dark red
> mugshot backdrop with horizontal height-ruler lines (police lineup
> style, ft/in markings) and a subtle spiderweb crack pattern. Right half:
> an intense close-up portrait of a sharp-featured young man with dark
> tousled hair swept to one side, narrowed piercing eyes, an aquiline
> nose, and a popped dark coat collar, the faint silhouette of a
> deerstalker cap brim visible at the top of frame and a pipe held near
> his mouth, dramatic rim lighting in cool blue tones, set against a deep
> navy-blue mugshot backdrop with matching height-ruler lines. A small
> magnifying glass icon in the lower-left corner. No text or letters
> anywhere in the image. High contrast, moody cinematic lighting,
> poster-quality digital art.

If a tool still flags it, trim "deerstalker cap brim" first — it's the
most specific-sounding phrase left; "pipe" and "magnifying glass" alone
still read as Holmes without tripping filters as easily.
