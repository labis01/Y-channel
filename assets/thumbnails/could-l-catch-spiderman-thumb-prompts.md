# Thumbnail prompts — "Could L Catch Spider-Man?"

Generated for use with an external AI image tool (Grok, ChatGPT/DALL-E, Midjourney,
Magnific, etc.) — this repo doesn't have image-generation access, so these are
meant to be pasted into whichever tool is used to produce the final art.

All concepts use the reference channel's format: split-face mugshot, height-ruler
lineup backdrop, warm/cool duotone per side. YouTube auto-adds the duration badge,
so none of these bake one in.

> cool grey-blue mugshot backdrop with matching height-ruler lines. Small black

## Concept 6 — Genericized (avoids copyright filters), matches v2 render
Text overlay: none

Grok and ChatGPT both rejected prompts that name the characters directly
("Spider-Man", "L", "Death Note") as copyright violations. This version
describes visual traits generically instead, so image generators don't
recognize it as a protected IP — matches the layout/hook of
`could-l-catch-spiderman-thumb-v2.png` (red string + gold case-meter ring).

> Photorealistic YouTube thumbnail, 1280x720, symmetrical split-face
> composition divided by a hard vertical white seam down the center. Left
> half: a close-up of a solid red full-face mask with large white
> lens-shaped eyes and fine black web-line texture across the surface, set
> against a dark red mugshot backdrop with horizontal height-ruler lines
> (police lineup style, ft/in markings) and a subtle spiderweb pattern.
> Right half: a close-up of a pale, tired-looking young man with messy
> black hair, dark circles under his eyes, and a hunched posture, wearing
> a plain dark long-sleeve top, set against a cool grey-blue mugshot
> backdrop with matching height-ruler lines. A small plain black
> rectangular notebook sits in the lower-left corner. A thin red string
> runs diagonally from the notebook across the seam to a point near the
> young man's head, like a detective's case board. A small circular
> meter/ring in gold, mostly filled, sits at the top center of the seam.
> No text or letters anywhere in the image. High contrast, dramatic
> cinematic lighting, poster-quality digital art.

If a tool still flags it, try trimming further: drop "detective's case
board" (implies a known trope but not a specific IP) or soften "notebook"
to "small black book."
